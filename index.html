Instructions

This is an orienteering game.

Your task is to guide the runner to control 13 as quickly as possible. The position cloud on the map represents uncertainty about the runner's position.

Choose the direction and distance of the next move (see Movement settings below). The effect on the position cloud is previewed on the map. When you commit, the runner moves roughly as you requested, with some randomness.

After each movement, the position cloud may shrink as the runner observes the terrain and localizes.

On long moves, the runner’s typical pace is about 4:45 per kilometre. Each move includes 3 seconds of map reading, so shorter moves are slower overall. The actual pace depends on how far the runner happens to go.

The terrain is perfectly flat forest. On the map, white represents forest and black dots are boulders.
